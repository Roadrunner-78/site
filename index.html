Billing and cancellation details

Prepaid from your agreed start date. Month to month; no minimum term. Renewal falls on the same date each month, or the month's last day if that date is absent, returning to the original date when available.

Service runs in seven-day blocks from each paid period's start, with a final partial block if needed. Cancellation ends service after the current block, or sooner by agreement. We refund every unused day afterward at the paid fee divided by the days in that billing period. The same calculation applies if we end it.

Example: after 14 used days in a 31-day period, the refund is $4,000 × 17/31 = $2,193.55.
