03 / The five milestones

You show yourself you can do it.

Four milestones you pay for. The fifth is where the paying stops. Each ends in something you do on real work, without my hands on the keyboard.

  1. 01

    You stop pasting.

    Get an answer only your own files could supply. Then break a real file and restore it yourself. If your work lives in Word, I explain the limits up front.

  2. 02

    You stop repeating yourself.

    Write a rule in your own words, rerun the job and watch the mistake stay fixed. Name one job you've decided to keep outside the system.

  3. 03

    You do one alone.

    Finish a real job without me and deliver it without asking me to check it. The person waiting for it uses it. Then do a different kind of job.

  4. 04

    You keep it true.

    Show me a stale file, broken reference or conflicting rule you found and repaired without asking. If it hasn't happened yet, you check your own system in a session. Anyone working with you gets involved too.

  5. 05

    You don't need me.

    No ending fee. No next monthly invoice. You keep the account, files and history; my access is removed the day you say so. Hourly help afterward, only by agreement, with no standing call. I'll raise the ending first.

There's no fixed calendar. Different parts of your work can be at different stages. We finish when you can get through the list on work I've never seen.
