03 / Selected work

My own work, with its limits.

These projects support my ability to build and check systems. They do not prove that paying clients became independent.

Investor deck

247 numbers traced to a source.

A program reads the finished deck and traces all 247 numbers across 36 slides back to a line in a lawyer-written offering memorandum. Unsourced numbers: zero. If that changes, the build stops.

Family work, not a paying client. This checks sourcing, not whether the source itself is right.

Cattle operation

83 terms grounded in operator knowledge.

The AI was confidently wrong. Twelve conversations with operators, an agronomist and a founder became a Spanish glossary of 83 terms, including what a rancher actually says and where I can get it wrong. Regional differences tell me to stop and ask. I'm still building the operation.

Property search

823 properties. Reasons to reject them.

The same system tracks 823 properties, including 216 mentioned by brokers rather than listed. Of 20 candidates screened, 11 were rejected; nine survived with concerns. It drafts WhatsApp messages and loads the right chat. It never sends. I do.

Market research

Five of my ideas shelved.

Sixteen years of futures data, about 16 billion records. I reconstructed missing trade-side data and checked it against later years with known answers. It matched every trade on the days checked, including a March 2020 day with roughly 750,000 trades. All five of my strategies were shelved.

05 / The five milestones

Hire me to unhire me.

Independence is something you demonstrate. The first four milestones guide the paid work. The fifth ends the monthly service.

  1. 1

    You stop pasting.

    Complete a task only your own files could support. Then break and restore a file yourself.

  2. 2

    You stop repeating yourself.

    Save a correction in your own words and check it on representative examples. Choose one task to keep outside the system. Saved rules don't guarantee error-free AI.

  3. 3

    You do one alone.

    Finish a real job without my check; its recipient uses it. Then complete a different kind of job under the same conditions.

  4. 4

    You keep it true.

    Find and repair stale material, a broken reference or conflicting instructions. If none arises, run the maintenance check in a session.

  5. 5

    You don't need me.

    No ending fee or next monthly invoice. You keep the files, accounts and history; my access is removed when we finish or whenever you request. Hourly help afterward is by agreement. I'll raise the ending first.

No fixed completion calendar. We review unfinished work before renewal; unused service remains refundable under the cancellation terms.
